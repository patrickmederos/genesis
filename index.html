About

I build the teams that keep autonomy running.

I've spent more than a decade making service operations excellent — in dealerships, at a mobile-mechanic startup, and across Tesla's service network. My work lives where customer experience meets operational rigor: keeping fleets moving, resolving issues before they escalate, and building the workflows that let teams scale.

Today I'm Tesla's Robotaxi / Cybercab Operations Supervisor, leading a mixed team of fleet support specialists, operations advisors, and technicians. I came to it the long way — I was the program's first Operations Advisor, helping shape the playbooks and standards the role runs on, including the job description for the people who'd come next. Now I'm the one coaching them.

What I care about is unglamorous and it is the whole job: a vehicle that's clean, charged, and ready when a rider opens the door. Recently certified in Artificial Intelligence, I'm focused on where autonomy and human-centered service meet.
